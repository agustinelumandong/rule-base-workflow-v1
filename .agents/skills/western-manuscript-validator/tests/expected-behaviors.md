# Western Manuscript Validator — Expected Behaviors

A case passes when the validator satisfies every **Must** item, avoids every **Must not** item, and gives a verdict consistent with the evidence. Exact prose is not required.

For historical cases, the confidence label is part of the required behavior. For layer cases, one primary layer is mandatory even when secondary effects are discussed.

## Input Classification

### CLASS-OUTLINE

**Mode:** Outline Mode
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Judge premise, causality, arc, escalation, chapter turns, antagonist logic, historical plausibility, and ending payoff.
- Treat the bullets as planning material.

**Must not:**
- Criticize sentence rhythm, grammar, or finished dialogue not supplied.
- Demand a saloon, arrival, or shootout.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Outline validation with structural findings only.

### CLASS-SCENE

**Mode:** Scene Mode
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Test Goal → Conflict → Outcome → Change.
- Assess necessity, tactical clarity, and what the public exposure changes.

**Must not:**
- Infer a full-book arc or series continuity.
- Treat the saloon setting as proof of Western quality.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Scene-level readiness verdict.

### CLASS-CHAPTER

**Mode:** Chapter Mode
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Check inherited wound and map, three turning beats, decision transfer, ending turn, and transition.
- Identify both informational and responsibility change.

**Must not:**
- Penalize missing full-book resolution.
- Ignore the prior-chapter consequence supplied.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Chapter-level verdict with `What Changed`.

### CLASS-MANUSCRIPT

**Mode:** Full-Manuscript Mode
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Use Pass One for story/structure before Pass Two for execution.
- Include applicable numeric scores and a readiness verdict.

**Must not:**
- Lead with grammar while central structure remains untested.
- Omit ending, aftermath, antagonist, or historical review.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Full report with scores.

### CLASS-SERIES

**Mode:** Series Mode
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Track the knee injury, witness promise, stage-company debt, damaged trust, elapsed time, and routes.
- Test whether prior growth survives pressure.

**Must not:**
- Invent Book 1 facts beyond the supplied summary.
- Treat an unknown as a contradiction.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Series-continuity report with continuity flags.

### CLASS-HISTORY

**Mode:** Historical-Authenticity Question Mode
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Separate firearm, cartridge, and travel-time questions.
- Use confidence labels and identify missing route variables.

**Must not:**
- Give a full story-readiness verdict when no story material exists.
- Claim exact travel feasibility without route evidence.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Bounded historical assessment; likely verification is required for at least part of the question.

### CLASS-DIALOGUE

**Mode:** Dialogue-Only Mode
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Judge ownership, friction, subtext, clarity, syntax, and period register to the extent supported.
- State the limitation created by missing action and context.

**Must not:**
- Score full plot, antagonist logic, or series continuity.
- Assume clipped dialogue is automatically successful.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Dialogue-only assessment with unsupported categories omitted.

## Primary Problem Layer

### LAYER-STORY

**Mode:** Chapter or excerpt review
**Primary layer:** Story
**Must:**
- State that the identification lacks a physical or informational basis.
- Repair the event by establishing a usable identifying clue, prior knowledge, or a less certain accusation.

**Must not:**
- Treat this mainly as prose or tone.
- Duplicate the same defect under several primary layers.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Major or Critical Story problem; not ready in current form.

### LAYER-PROSE

**Mode:** Excerpt review
**Primary layer:** Prose
**Must:**
- Recognize that the escape sequence can work logically.
- Identify ambiguous pronouns and unclear positions as the main failure.
- Prescribe a clearer subject and spatial sequence.

**Must not:**
- Rebuild the underlying event as a story repair.
- Call the escape physically impossible without evidence.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Focused prose revision.

### LAYER-TONE

**Mode:** Dialogue or chapter review
**Primary layer:** Tone
**Must:**
- Identify the modern therapeutic register as unsupported by year and characterization.
- Preserve the coherent underlying conflict.

**Must not:**
- Claim emotional directness itself is non-Western.
- Treat the problem primarily as grammar.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Major Tone revision.

### LAYER-CONTINUITY

**Mode:** Chapter review with prior material
**Primary layer:** Continuity
**Must:**
- Compare each action with the established hand limits and cold-weather numbness.
- Require recovery, support, altered technique, consequence, or a different action.

**Must not:**
- Call the prior-book note unknown.
- Treat the issue only as action prose.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Critical or Major Continuity problem.

### LAYER-PACING

**Mode:** Chapter-transition review
**Primary layer:** Pacing
**Must:**
- Identify the skipped burial, return, accusation, evidence handling, and family rupture as crossed consequences.
- Recommend a bridge or aftermath scene that contains decisions and change.

**Must not:**
- Demand routine travel filler.
- treat the death itself as the problem.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Major Pacing revision.

## Change Detection

### CHANGE-QUIET

**Mode:** Scene Mode
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Identify relational change: trust increases.
- Identify responsibility change: route authority and ledger custody transfer.
- Recognize admission of limitation as behavioral movement.

**Must not:**
- Call the scene weak merely because it lacks action.
- Demand an external threat.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Functionally strong quiet scene if execution supports it.

### CHANGE-ACTION-FLAT

**Mode:** Scene Mode
**Primary layer:** Pacing
**Must:**
- State that activity occurs but no meaningful story change follows.
- Ask what cost, information, delay, identity exposure, injury, or decision the attack creates.

**Must not:**
- Praise the scene solely for action.
- Count unnamed deaths as sufficient change when nothing downstream shifts.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Needs focused revision or removal.

### CHANGE-RECAP

**Mode:** Scene Mode
**Primary layer:** Pacing
**Must:**
- Identify the scene as functionally flat.
- Recommend adding disagreement, new evidence, a changed assignment, a cost, or cutting/compressing it.

**Must not:**
- Label repeated information as necessary exposition without pressure.
- Rewrite the conversation.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Cut, compress, or add a turn.

### CHANGE-AFTERMATH

**Mode:** Scene Mode
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Identify leadership transfer and debt as valid change.
- Recognize the burial as aftermath with forward consequence.

**Must not:**
- Call it passive because it occurs after a death.
- Require gunplay.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Strong aftermath function if causally prepared.

## Causality and Antagonist Logic

### CAUSE-FRAME-PLAUSIBLE

**Mode:** Story-logic review
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Trace access → copied heel/tin/coat → planting → laundress discovery path → false inference → exposure risk.
- Note that multiple witnesses create useful contradiction risk.

**Must not:**
- Call the plan flawless.
- ignore how timing could expose the owner.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Plausible frame-up with identified risks to manage.

### CAUSE-FRAME-SELFEXPOSE

**Mode:** Story-logic review
**Primary layer:** Story
**Must:**
- Flag the custom spur as direct self-exposure.
- Ask why the matching spur and lack of theft report would not point back to Harrow.
- Prescribe a planted item plausibly tied to the drifter or a credible theft chain.

**Must not:**
- Accept the narration's claim that the plan is brilliant.
- Solve the issue through wording alone.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Major Story repair.

### CAUSE-BETRAYAL-NOACCESS

**Mode:** Story-logic review
**Primary layer:** Story
**Must:**
- Flag missing knowledge/access before debating motive.
- Require an informant, overheard cue, observed preparation, route inference, or changed betrayer.

**Must not:**
- Assume the blacksmith somehow learned it.
- Treat secrecy as sufficient setup.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Critical causal gap.

### CAUSE-COINCIDENCE-SOLUTION

**Mode:** Story-logic or ending review
**Primary layer:** Story
**Must:**
- Flag coincidence resolving the central conflict.
- Move the witness onto an established route, message chain, pursuit, relationship, or deliberate choice.

**Must not:**
- Treat lamplight as adequate setup.
- excuse decisive coincidence because Western towns receive travelers.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Structural revision to the climax.

### CAUSE-SABOTAGE-HELPS

**Mode:** Antagonist-logic review
**Primary layer:** Story
**Must:**
- Ask what the saboteur gains from each act.
- Flag that the attacks impose no cost and clarify identity.
- Require adaptation, real damage, misdirection, or a different objective.

**Must not:**
- Call repeated sabotage escalation merely because the methods differ.
- ignore the antagonist's failure to change strategy.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Major antagonist-plan revision.

## Character and Dialogue

### VOICE-SAME-RHYTHM

**Mode:** Dialogue-Only or chapter review
**Primary layer:** Prose
**Must:**
- Flag shared syntax and rhetorical structure across four distinct speakers.
- Differentiate by occupation, education, age, status, relationship, and objective.
- Cap Dialogue at 6 if scoring is requested.

**Must not:**
- Praise all lines as economical Western dialogue.
- solve differentiation only with dialect spelling.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Focused dialogue revision.

### VOICE-DIALECT-CONSISTENT

**Mode:** Dialogue-Only or chapter review
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Recognize the speech as intentional, clear, occupationally grounded, and speaker-specific.
- Preserve rough grammar unless it creates ambiguity.

**Must not:**
- Correct it into formal standard English merely because it is rough.
- assume dialect is automatically authentic without year/region context.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Dialogue is acceptable on the supplied evidence.

### VOICE-EXPOSITION

**Mode:** Dialogue review
**Primary layer:** Prose
**Must:**
- Flag shared-history exposition written for the reader.
- Prescribe friction, incomplete knowledge, accusation, correction, or removal.

**Must not:**
- treat quotation marks as proof of natural dialogue.
- rewrite the exchange unless separately requested.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Major dialogue/prose repair.

### VOICE-PREACHER-LONG

**Mode:** Dialogue or scene review
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Judge ownership, purpose, pressure, era fit, and consequence.
- Recognize that the long cadence is distinct and changes the crew's decision.

**Must not:**
- Cut the speech solely because Western dialogue should be short.
- force the preacher into drover syntax.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Potentially strong dialogue, subject to tone and historical context.

### VOICE-THOUGHT-DUPLICATE

**Mode:** Prose review
**Primary layer:** Prose
**Must:**
- Identify the final sentence as duplicating visible fear.
- Recommend cutting it or replacing it with new information/choice.

**Must not:**
- ban interiority globally.
- call every use of “knew” wrong.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Minor or Moderate prose repair.

## Historical Confidence

### HIST-CONFIRMED-WEAPON

**Mode:** Historical-Authenticity Question Mode
**Primary layer:** Tone
**Must:**
- Use **Confirmed issue** because supplied dates directly conflict.
- State that story impact is major or critical because the weapon is central.

**Must not:**
- require outside research to notice the supplied contradiction.
- treat the fictional name as immunity from the dates the author supplied.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Replace, redates, or deliberately fictionalize the setting/technology.

### HIST-PLAUSIBLE-WORD

**Mode:** Historical-Authenticity Question Mode
**Primary layer:** Tone
**Must:**
- Use **Verification required** or **Historically plausible**.
- Distinguish a character-specific metaphor from a claimed period idiom.

**Must not:**
- use **Confirmed issue** without evidence.
- reject the phrase merely because it is unfamiliar.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Keep provisionally or verify intended usage.

### HIST-TRAVEL-UNKNOWN

**Mode:** Historical-Authenticity Question Mode
**Primary layer:** Story
**Must:**
- Use **Verification required**.
- Name missing distance, route, terrain, weather, water, horse condition, remounts, and rest.

**Must not:**
- confirm or reject thirty-six hours from intuition alone.
- invent the locations.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Cannot validate until route assumptions are supplied.

### HIST-INSTITUTION-CORRECT

**Mode:** Historical-Authenticity Question Mode
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Use **Historically plausible** on the supplied research note.
- Recognize the jurisdiction distinction.

**Must not:**
- manufacture a legal problem not present in the source.
- demand a courtroom scene.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** No historical flag on supplied facts.

### HIST-PULP-SIMPLIFICATION

**Mode:** Historical-Authenticity Question or manuscript sub-check
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Treat the omission as acceptable genre simplification unless it breaks custody, causality, or physical possibility.
- Judge story impact rather than procedural completeness.

**Must not:**
- require every form and hearing on-page.
- lower historical score solely for compressed minor procedure.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Historically plausible/acceptable simplification.

## Repair Quality and Scoring

### REPAIR-VAGUE

**Mode:** Chapter review
**Primary layer:** Pacing
**Must:**
- Replace “add tension” with a specific source of resistance or cost.
- Identify options such as conflicting testimony, witness demand, route loss, accusation, or changed conclusion.

**Must not:**
- repeat vague advice.
- add arbitrary gunmen unrelated to the chapter's purpose.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Needs a focused revision.

### REPAIR-STRUCTURAL

**Mode:** Outline review
**Primary layer:** Story
**Must:**
- Prescribe the missing planted object, access chain, witness position, discovery mechanism, false inference, and contradiction risk.
- Keep the repair structural rather than drafting prose.

**Must not:**
- say only “make the frame-up clearer.”
- invent a complete replacement scene unless requested.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Needs structural revision.

### SCORE-POLISHED-BROKEN

**Mode:** Full-outline, chapter, or manuscript scoring
**Primary layer:** Story
**Must:**
- Cap Story and Plot at 5.
- Let strong prose score separately.
- Give a verdict no higher than Needs structural revision.

**Must not:**
- average elegant prose into a passing overall verdict.
- score coincidence and access failures as minor.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Needs structural revision or Not ready.

### SCORE-ROUGH-STRONG

**Mode:** Full-outline, chapter, or manuscript scoring
**Primary layer:** Prose
**Must:**
- Give fair, potentially strong story/logic/pacing scores.
- Score grammar/readability separately and identify local polish needs.

**Must not:**
- reduce all categories because grammar is rough.
- call the material publication-ready without acknowledging execution repairs.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Good with minor repairs or Needs a focused revision, depending on roughness.

### SCORE-NOCHANGE

**Mode:** Chapter Mode with scores
**Primary layer:** Pacing
**Must:**
- Cap Pacing and Beats at 5.
- State that polish and research do not replace a chapter turn.

**Must not:**
- give a high pacing score for atmosphere alone.
- invent a change not present.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Needs a focused revision.

### SCORE-NONAPPLICABLE

**Mode:** Dialogue-Only Mode with scores
**Primary layer:** Not applicable
**Must:**
- Score only supported categories such as Dialogue, Western Tone, Grammar/Readability, and perhaps Prose Clarity.
- Omit Series Continuity, Story and Plot, Antagonist Logic, and Historical Authenticity when the year is unknown.

**Must not:**
- assign zeros to unsupported categories.
- infer a full story from one page.

**Expected verdict or disposition:** Bounded dialogue scorecard with limitations stated.
