# Chapter Preflight Checklist

Run before submitting any chapter draft for review. Apply to all books in the series.

## 1. Continuity

- [ ] All time references (weeks, days, hours) checked against source chronology. No contradictions.
- [ ] All object-state changes tracked: hats picked up, needles set down, doors opened/closed. Nothing moves twice without cause.
- [ ] Character positions match last chapter's continuity-out. Opening physical positions are correct.
- [ ] No background characters lingering across scene breaks unless explicitly re-established.
- [ ] Witness count and positions are internally consistent (who saw what from where).

## 2. Physical/Sensory Logic

- [ ] Temperature, time of day, and visible effects (breath, sweat, dust, shadows) are consistent within the scene.
- [ ] Sound propagation is plausible (gunshot in enclosed room vs open air, hoofbeats at distance).
- [ ] Mechanical descriptions use consistent terminology throughout (same brake mechanism, same tool reference).

## 3. POV Discipline

- [ ] Every observation traceable to what the POV character can see, hear, smell, or touch at that moment.
- [ ] No cause-of-condition statements the POV character could not know.
    - BAD: "His lower back was stiff from years on a wagon box."
    - GOOD: "He straightened slowly, one hand pressed to his lower back."
- [ ] New characters are described by appearance first, named only after the POV character learns their name (someone speaks it, or the POV character recognizes them).
- [ ] No internal monologue. No "He knew," "He understood," "He realized," "He felt," "He thought."

## 4. Grammar and Mechanics

- [ ] Zero em dashes anywhere in the manuscript.
- [ ] Compound adjectives are hyphenated: flat-brimmed hat, hand-stitched boots, six-horse teams, flat-footed landing, smooth-faced boy, leather-bound ledger.
- [ ] Pronoun references are unambiguous. Every "he" has a clear antecedent in the same or preceding sentence.
- [ ] Sentence fragments exist only where intentional. Run-on fragments checked.

## 5. Dialogue Tags

- [ ] Zero "said," "asked," "shouted," or other speech tags. All dialogue attribution uses action anchors.
- [ ] Speaker is clear in every exchange. Alternating pattern anchored at least once per exchange.
- [ ] Action anchors attached to the correct speaker (not the listener).

## 6. Beat Density

- [ ] Every source-listed beat is present in the prose.
- [ ] Beat word counts are within elastic range. Shortfalls are filled with source-authorized substance (terrain, labor, sensory detail, character physicality), not filler or repetition.
- [ ] No beat is padded to hit a uniform length. Short beats are valid for setup/aftermath. Long beats are justified by major conflict/reveal/climax.

## 7. AI Slop Check

- [ ] No banned echo words: absolutely, completely, relentless, massive, sharp, heavy, pure, extremely, perfectly.
- [ ] No modern/clinical terms: velocity, fraction, trajectory, impact, visible, resolving.
- [ ] No "the kind that," "the way that," or explanatory clauses restating what the action already showed.
- [ ] No similes or metaphors unless explicitly permitted.
- [ ] No personification of inanimate objects (sun climbed, wind whispered, room knew).

## 8. Source Lock

- [ ] No invented facts, backstories, motives, or relationships not present in phase-0.md or chapter-summaries.md.
- [ ] No character names, dialogue, or outcomes invented beyond the source.
- [ ] Research-pack terms used in prose are documented in research-pack.md.

## 9. Banned Element Check

- [ ] No institutional villain, resource-rights scheme, or trial scene.
- [ ] No new-character names that collide with settings.json banned_names list (returning characters exempt).
- [ ] No "Voss" as any name anywhere.

## 10. Chapter Handoff

- [ ] Chapter opens with immediate movement from prior chapter's endpoint. No time gap unless source-specified.
- [ ] Chapter ends at the outline-specified beat. No extra reaction beats bleeding into the next chapter.
- [ ] Continuity-out updated with concrete details (not planning stubs) from the drafted prose.
